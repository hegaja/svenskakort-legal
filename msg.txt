Drop the caveats section and say what happens to a tester's email

Removes the "Honest caveats" section at the owner's request.

Adds a retention promise to the tester box: the address is used only to add
someone to the Play test and to reply to them, is never shared or added to a
list, and is deleted when testing ends. Someone is being asked to hand over a
Google account email to a stranger, and saying up front how long it is kept is
the difference between a request and an imposition.

Consistent with the privacy policy, which already says an address sent by email
is used to answer and for nothing else. This is narrower and time-bound, so it
adds a promise rather than contradicting one.
