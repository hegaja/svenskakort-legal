Turn the index into a page that recruits testers

The index was a two-line list of legal documents. It is now the app's page,
because there is one thing it can usefully do right now: find the testers Google
Play requires before an app can go public. That is the actual bottleneck, and a
page is a better thing to hand someone than a paragraph of explanation.

Written as a recruiting page rather than a marketing one. For a niche app almost
every install comes from search inside the Play Store, not from the web, so a
landing page that claims to be an acquisition funnel would be pretending. What it
can be is a link worth sending to a person.

Carries the honest caveats too - that no native speaker has checked the deck, and
that progress does not follow you to a new phone - because someone deciding
whether to spend two weeks testing deserves to know both before they start, not
after.
